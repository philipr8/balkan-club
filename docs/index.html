Who We Are

Celebrating Balkan Culture & Community

We are a vibrant and inclusive community celebrating the rich cultures, histories, and traditions of the Balkan region right here at Queen's University.

From traditional music and dance to discussions about history and geopolitics, we bring together students with Balkan roots — and anyone who wants to learn about this remarkable corner of the world.

Our family spans Macedonia, Croatia, Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Slovenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Albania, Greece, Kosovo, and Montenegro. Come as you are — you belong here.

Cultural Events

Celebrating Balkan traditions through food, music, dance, and festivals that bring our heritage to life.

Social Gatherings

Bar nights, potlucks, watchparties — the kind of warmth that defines Balkan hospitality.

Educational Initiatives

Exploring the history, politics, and culture of southeastern Europe — shaped by empires and rich in identity.

Our Events

Balkan Potluck

Share traditional food from across the Balkans in a warm, family-style gathering. Burek, ajvar, baklava — all welcome.

Football Watchparties

Cheer on your Balkan national teams together. There's nothing like watching the game surrounded by people who feel it as deeply as you do.

Bar Nights

Casual socials to connect, unwind, and celebrate Balkan spirit. Good company, great energy.
